💡 Frequently Asked Questions
1. How do I set or update my monthly target budget? ▼
You can set your budget from the home screen by clicking the Set Budget button on the Total Budget card, or navigate to Change Budget from the Quick Actions row. Enter your target amount in Indian Rupees (₹) and optionally allocate limits across specific categories like Food, Utilities, Transport, and Shopping.
2. How do I record a new expense? ▼
Click Add Expense on the home screen and fill in the description, amount, category, date, and an optional note. Once saved, it immediately updates your remaining budget and Monthly Summary.
3. What is the "Portal to Bunnies" and how do I unlock them? ▼
The Portal to Bunnies is Pocket Bunny's gamified savings sanctuary! Everyone starts with a locked sanctuary. Each month you log expenses and finish the month within your total budget, you unlock the next of 7 collectible bunnies, starting with the Sprout Carrot Bunny. A month counts once it has ended. Check your sanctuary progress at any time by clicking the 🐰 Portal to Bunnies button in the header.
4. Can I view, filter, or delete previous transactions? ▼
Yes! Go to Transaction History from the Quick Actions row. You can filter your expenses by category, date range, and minimum or maximum amount, edit any entry, or delete mistakes with a single click.
5. How do I change my profile information or password? ▼
Click ⚙ Settings in the top header, then tap Edit Profile to update your name and email. To set a new password, use the Change button under Account Settings.
6. Where is my financial and expense data stored? ▼
Your expense records and budget targets are stored in your Pocket Bunny account on the server, so they are there whenever you sign in. Passwords are stored only as secure hashes. You can delete individual expenses from Transaction History, or delete your whole account from the Danger Zone in Settings.
7. How does the Monthly Summary page work? ▼
The Monthly Summary provides an instant financial breakdown of your total budget, total spent, and remaining savings. It also shows a category-by-category visual progress bar to help you see exactly where your money went. Use the month picker at the top to look back at earlier months.
